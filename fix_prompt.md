# 📋 Fire Safety Inspection Checklist: Redesign & Fix Specification

> **Document Version:** 1.0.0  
> **Source Standard:** `Fire Safety Inspection Checklist.docx` (BFP-QSF-FSED-061 Rev. 00 / Official Bureau of Fire Protection Standard)  
> **Target Platforms:** Flutter Mobile Application (`firesight_mobile`), Supabase Database, PDF Export Engine  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Goals

### 1.1 Problem Statement
The current Fire Safety Inspection feature in the Fire Inspector account suffers from two major limitations:
1. **Export Discrepancies & Data Omission:** The exported document generated by `InspectionChecklistPdfService.dart` diverges from `Fire Safety Inspection Checklist.docx`. Multiple dimensions and fields (e.g., exit widths, travel distances, illumination lux/time, specific itemized defects, and checkmarks) are either missing, hardcoded as blank underlines, or improperly mapped from user inputs.
2. **Monolithic Form UX:** The mobile checklist form (`commercial_checklist_widget.dart` / `commercial_inspection_form_screen.dart`) is currently a massive, single continuous scroll view (1,800+ lines). In contrast, the app's House-to-House (H2H) and Community Fire Protection Plan (CFPP) surveys feature intuitive, multi-step wizards with progress indicators, sticky headers, step-by-step navigation, and distinct cards.

### 1.2 Objectives
1. **Step-by-Step Wizard Redesign:** Transform the commercial fire safety inspection form into a modern **6-Step Interactive Wizard** modeled directly after H2H (`HouseToHouseChecklistScreen`) and CFPP (`BarangayRiskSurveyScreen`).
2. **100% Comprehensive Coverage of DOCX Standard:** Extract and embed every single checklist item, table, dimension field, and specification from `Fire Safety Inspection Checklist.docx`.
3. **Pixel-Perfect Export Fidelity:** Overhaul `InspectionChecklistPdfService.dart` so that the generated document mirrors `Fire Safety Inspection Checklist.docx` in structure, tables, fonts, borders, headers, and footers, with all entered data dynamically populated.
4. **Supabase Schema & Offline Sync Alignment:** Ensure seamless synchronization with Supabase's `public.inspections` table and `checklist_data` JSONB structure, supporting offline draft caching and audit logging.

## 2. Comprehensive Analysis of `Fire Safety Inspection Checklist.docx`

The official document contains **11 core sections** across 10 official pages:

### Section I: REFERENCE
* **Inspection Order No. (IO):** Text string (e.g., `IO-2026-004-R1`)
* **Date Issued:** Date (`YYYY-MM-DD`)
* **Date Inspected:** Date (`YYYY-MM-DD`)

### Section II: NATURE OF INSPECTION CONDUCTED
* Multiple-choice checkboxes:
  1. `[ ] Inspection during construction`
  2. `[ ] FSIC for Certificate of Annual Inspection (PEZA)`
  3. `[ ] FSIC for Certificate for Occupancy`
  4. `[ ] FSIC for Business Permit (New/Renewal)`
  5. `[ ] Verification Inspection for Compliance:`
     * Sub-options: `[ ] NTC` | `[ ] NTCV` | `[ ] Abatement` | `[ ] Closure`
  6. `[ ] Others (Specify):` Text input

### Section III: REQUIREMENTS
* `FSIC for Occupancy:`
  * Fire Safety Compliance and Commissioning Report (FSCCR) (if applicable): `Yes [ ] / No [ ]`
* `FSIC for New / Renewal / Annual Inspection / Others:`
  * Fire Safety Maintenance Report (FSMR) (if applicable): `Yes [ ] / No [ ]`

### Section IV: GENERAL INFORMATION
* **Establishment Profile:**
  * Name of Building
  * Address
  * Business Name
  * Nature of Business
  * Name of Owner / Representative
  * Contact No.
* **Occupancy Permit History:**
  * FSEC No. & Date Issued
  * Building Permit No. & Date Issued
* **Renewal / Annual History:**
  * FSIC No. (Latest) & Date Issued
  * Certificate of Fire Drill No. & Date Issued
  * Business Permit No. & Date Issued
  * Fire Insurance Policy No. (if any) & Date Issued
* **Construction Type:**
  * `[ ] Type I : Concrete & Steel (Fire Resistive)`
  * `[ ] Type II : Concrete & Exposed Steel (Noncombustible)`
  * `[ ] Type III : Concrete & Wood (Ordinary)`
  * `[ ] Type IV : Heavy Timber (Large mass wood)`
  * `[ ] Type V : Wood frame (Lightweight wood)`
* **Interior Finish (Walls / Ceiling):**
  * `[ ] Class A (Flame spread index 0–25, smoke developed 0–450)`
  * `[ ] Class B (Flame spread index 26–75, smoke developed 0–450)`
  * `[ ] Class C (Flame spread index 76–200, smoke developed 0–450)`
* **Interior Finish (Floor):**
  * `[ ] Class I (Critical radiant flux >= 0.45 W/cm²)`
  * `[ ] Class II (Critical radiant flux 0.22 – 0.45 W/cm²)`
* **Sectional Occupancy (Specific usage per floor):**
  * Basement, Ground Floor, 2nd Floor, 3rd Floor, 4th Floor, Nth Floor
* **General Occupancy Classification:**
  * Assembly, Educational, Day Care, Health Care, Detention and Correctional, Residential, Residential Board and Care, Mercantile, Business, Industrial, Storage, Special Structure
* **Other Information:**
  * Maximum Occupant Load (`P/Floor`)
  * Number of Stories (`Storey`)
  * Building Height (`m`)
  * Highrise: `Yes [ ] / No [ ]`

### Section V: MEANS OF EGRESS
* **A. Exit Access:**
  * Checkboxes: Doors, Corridors, Hallways, Passageways, Anterooms, Ramps
  * Horizontal Components Table (Actual Dimensions `m`, Passed/Failed, Remarks):
    1. Doors
    2. Corridors / Hallways
    3. Passageways
    4. Lobby / Anteroom
    5. Ramps
    6. Common path of travel
    7. Dead end
    8. Travel distance
  * Exit Access Requirements Table (Actual Dim, Passed, Failed):
    1. Door leaf leaves not less than 1/2 of required width unobstructed
    2. Door leaf projection <= 180 mm into required width
    3. At least 2 means of egress for room load >= 50 or hazard content
    4. Guest door >= 20 mins fire resistant
    5. Doors opening onto exit access corridors are self-closing & self-latching
    6. No openings in corridor partitions other than door openings
    7. Free from any obstruction
    8. No flammable material stored
* **B. Exits:**
  * Types: Normal Stairs, Curved Stairs, Spiral Stairs, Winding Stairs, Horizontal Exits, Outside Stairs, Exit Passageways, Fire Escape Stairs, Fire Escape Ladder, Slide Escape
  * Components Table (Clear Width `m`, Passed/Failed, Remarks):
    1. Exits Doors
    2. Normal Stairs
    3. Curve stairs
    4. Winding Stairs
    5. Horizontal Exits
    6. Outside Stairs
    7. Exit Passageways
    8. Fire Escape Stairs
    9. Fire Escape Ladders
    10. Slide Escape
  * Exit Specifications Table (Actual Dim, Passed, Failed):
    1. At least 2 means of egress for each floor
    2. Doors assembly: 60 mins fire resistant (<= 3 levels)
    3. Doors assembly: 90 mins fire resistant (>= 4 levels)
    4. Exit Doors with re-entry mechanism at every 4 storey
    5. Stair tread: Min depth = 280 mm
    6. Stair riser: Height = 100 mm to 180 mm
    7. Minimum stair headroom: 2000 mm
    8. Stair provided with Guard and Handrails
    9. Maximum handrails projection: 114 mm
    10. Stair landing >= required width of exit door
    11. Exit doors open and close properly
    12. Doors swing in direction of egress
    13. Exit doors with panic hardware, vision panel, and self-closing
    14. No enclosed usable space under stairs
    15. Interior finish: Class B
* **C. Exits Discharge Table (Actual Dim, Passed, Failed):**
  1. Remoteness of exit discharge >= 1/2 length of overall dimension
  2. Remoteness of exit discharge >= 1/3 length if protected by ASASS
  3. Exterior grounds clear of objects impeding evacuation/equipment
  4. Terminate directly at a public way or exterior exit discharge

### Section VI: SIGNS, LIGHTING, AND EXITS SIGNAGE
* **A. Marking of Means of Egress (Exit):**
  1. Minimum letter height, 150 mm (Actual Dim, Passed/Failed)
  2. EXIT signs posted along Exit access, Exits, and Exit discharge (Passed/Failed)
  3. EXIT signs properly illuminated (Passed/Failed)
* **B. Marking of Means of Egress (Emergency Evacuation Plan):**
  * Table 1 (Passed/Failed): Strategic location, Photo-luminescent background with 12 standard items.
  * Table 2: Plan Sizes (Actual Dim, Passed/Failed):
    - Floor area < 50 m²: 330.2 mm x 215.9 mm
    - Floor area 50 – 150 m²: 609.6 mm x 457.2 mm
    - Floor area >= 151 m²: 609.6 mm x 914.4 mm
* **C. Illumination of Means of Egress (Actual Lux/Time, Passed/Failed):**
  1. Walking surfaces: >= 1 ft-candle (10.8 lux)
  2. Assembly exit access: >= 0.2 ft-candle (2.2 lux)
  3. Stairs: >= 10 ft-candle (108 lux)
  4. Emergency lighting average: >= 1 ft-candle (10.8 lux) / min 0.1 ft-candle (1.1 lux)
  5. Emergency lighting automatic duration: >= 1.5 hours
  6. Periodic testing with written records

### Section VII: HAZARD
* Header Table: Hazard Contents, Quantity (Vol./Weight), Placard, Within MAQ (Yes/No), Identification No., Classification (Low/Ordinary/High), Class, Flash Point.
* Subsections (Passed/Failed):
  * A. Other Flammable Liquids (3 items)
  * B. Miscellaneous Hazards (Mechanical room, Storage) (2 items)
  * C. Housekeeping, Maintenance, Storage & Waste Disposal (3 items)

### Section VIII: FIRE PROTECTION
* Subsections A to K:
  * A. Automatic Fire Suppression System (Sprinkler) (3 items)
  * B. Wet Standpipe / Fire Hose Cabinet (5 items)
  * C. Fire Pump (4 items)
  * D. Fire Detection System (1 item)
  * E. Fire Alarm Facilities (2 items)
  * F. Lifts / Elevators (3 items)
  * G. First Aid Fire Protection (Fire Extinguishers) (7 items)
  * H. Emergency Lighting Systems (1 item)
  * I. Kitchen (Hoods, Vents, Filters date) (2 items)
  * J. Building Service Equipment (Cooking AKHFSS, HVAC PMEC, Smoke Control, Chutes/Incinerators) (4 items)
  * K. Fire Wall (Provided Yes/No, Extension >= 760mm Yes/No, Wall Type: 125mm Solid Concrete / 150mm Solid Masonry / 200mm Hollow Masonry)

### Section IX: DEFECTS, RECOMMENDATIONS & SIGNATURES
* **Defects / Deficiencies:**
  * Itemized fields: ITEM IV, ITEM V, ITEM VI, ITEM VII, ITEM VIII
  * Photo attachments / sketches
* **Recommendations:**
  * Comply defects and pay fees for FSIC
  * Or for issuance of: Notice to Comply (NTC), Notice to Correct Violation (NTCV), Closure Order, Abatement Order with Fine, Closure Order for Non-Payment of Fine, Notice of Disapproval (NOD)
* **Signatures & Approvals:**
  * Acknowledged By: Owner / Representative, Fire Safety Inspector/s, Team Leader, Date & Time
  * Recommend Approval: Chief, Fire Safety Enforcement Section/Unit (FSES/FSED)
  * Approval: City / Municipal Fire Marshal

## 3. Step-by-Step Wizard Architecture (Flutter UI)

To replicate the clean, high-performance flow of `HouseToHouseChecklistScreen` and `BarangayRiskSurveyScreen`, the commercial inspection form will be divided into **6 logical steps**:

```
[Step 1: Reference & Profile]
  └── IO No., Nature of Inspection, Requirements, Business Profile, Permit History

[Step 2: Building Specs & Occupancy]
  └── Construction Type, Interior Finishes, Sectional Occupancy, General Classification, Height/Stories

[Step 3: Means of Egress]
  └── Exit Access (Horizontal components + 8 specs), Exits (Stairs + 15 specs), Exit Discharge

[Step 4: Signs, Lighting & Hazards]
  └── Egress Markings, Evac Plan Sizes, Lux/Time Illumination, Hazard Identification, Housekeeping

[Step 5: Fire Protection Systems]
  └── Sprinkler, Wet Standpipe, Pumps, Alarms, Elevators, Extinguishers, Kitchen, BSE, Fire Wall

[Step 6: Defects, Recommendations & Signatures]
  └── Itemized Defects (IV-VIII), Photo Attachments, Recommendations, 5 Signatory Approvals
```

### 3.1 Wizard Component Layout
Each step will feature:
1. **Sticky Header:**
   - Step indicator: `STEP X OF 6`
   - Step title
   - Live compliance summary badge
   - Smooth linear progress bar (`value: (currentStep + 1) / 6`)
2. **Scrollable Content Body:**
   - Modular section cards (`_buildSectionCard`) with custom BFP orange/navy accents
   - Clean segmented toggle buttons for `Passed`, `Failed`, and `N/A`
   - Numeric & decimal text fields for actual dimensions (`m`, `lux`, `hours`, `mm`)
   - Expandable photo attachment picker with local caching
3. **Sticky Bottom Navigation Bar:**
   - `Previous Step` button (disabled on Step 1)
   - `Next: [Next Step Title]` button with validation
   - `Submit Inspection` button on Step 6 with loading spinner and offline fallback

## 4. Export Service Redesign (`InspectionChecklistPdfService.dart`)

### 4.1 Identified Root Causes in Existing PDF Service
1. **Hardcoded Empty Underlines:**
   In lines 1127, 1333, 1420, 1464, 1584, 1613, 1640, 1688, 1719, and 1742, actual dimension values were rendered as empty boxes `pw.Container(...)` without pulling from `model.itemDimensions`.
2. **Missing Sectional Occupancy & Finish Bindings:**
   Certain checkboxes and values in Sectional Occupancy were missing fallback handling or did not format cleanly across folio page boundaries.
3. **Typography & Spacing Alignment:**
   Official BFP Form 061 requires 8.5 x 13 inch (Folio / Philippine Long Bond) format. Headers and footers must match the exact lettering and numbering `Page X of 10`.

### 4.2 Fix Implementation in PDF Generator
1. **Full Dynamic Dimension Binding:** Connect every table cell directly to `model.itemDimensions[itemKey]`.
2. **Dynamic Checkbox Tick Marks:** Ensure exact checkmark rendering using `pw.CustomPaint` or Unicode check symbols.
3. **Defects & Remarks Integration:** Ensure remarks from each section flow automatically into the defects table on Page 10.
4. **Signatures Block:** Accurately print all 5 signatory titles and names.

## 5. Supabase Schema & Data Synchronization

### 5.1 `public.inspections` Table Analysis
The table schema supports:
* `id` (UUID, Primary Key)
* `inspector_id` (UUID references `auth.users`)
* `establishment_id` (UUID, optional foreign key)
* `checklist_type` (`'commercial'`)
* `inspection_order_no` (VARCHAR)
* `date_issued` (DATE)
* `date_inspected` (TIMESTAMPTZ)
* `business_name` (VARCHAR)
* `address` (TEXT)
* `overall_status` (`'Completed'`, `'Pending Sync'`)
* `compliance_status` (VARCHAR)
* `recommendation` (TEXT)
* `risk_level` (VARCHAR)
* `score` (INTEGER)
* `rating` (VARCHAR)
* `checklist_data` (JSONB)
* `hazard_photo_urls` (JSONB)
* `created_at`, `updated_at`
